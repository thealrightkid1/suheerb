A special place
to grow.

For over forty years, our goal has been to give students the highest-quality martial arts training and philosophy. We give young people a head start with skills that build strength, intelligence and happiness, and teach adults self-defense that protects as well as it keeps them fit.

A chance to step forward

Developing
young leaders.

In our Student Leadership Program, students of every age and rank take on real roles in the school. Under the supervision of trained instructors they lead exercises, read aloud and help teach, building confidence that shows up everywhere else.

FAQ

Good
questions.

Do I need any martial arts experience?

Not at all. Most of our students start as complete beginners. Instructors teach every technique step by step, and you'll train alongside students at your level.

How young can my child start?

Our Pre-K Martial Arts program is designed especially for pre-schoolers. Older children join Youth Martial Arts or the After-School Program. Book a trial and we'll help you choose.

What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable workout clothes you can move in. You don't need a uniform or any equipment for your trial class.

How often should I train?

We recommend at least two classes a week plus a few minutes of practice at home. That's the pace where students see steady progress.

How do belt promotions work?

Each belt has its own goals and a minimum time at that rank. When your instructor sees you're ready, you're invited to test for your next belt.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Parents are welcome to watch, and many families train together.
